User Guide

Getting started

  1. Upload a defect file (CSV, TSV, or XLSX) at the top of the sidebar.
  2. Set Wafer Size, Die Size, and Notch Direction to match your data — before or after uploading, both work.
  3. Hover a die on the wafer map for its coordinate; click a die (or a dot on the die defect map) to highlight it. Ctrl/Cmd/Shift-click, or -drag a box, on either map to select multiple dies at once; click empty space to clear the selection.
  4. Use Wafer Center and GDS Origin Offset (with their auto-calibrate icons) if the map doesn't line up with your data.
  5. Click the full-view icon on the die defect map to inspect the defect pattern at a larger size.

File format

CSV, TSV or XLSX (.xlsx/.xls). Required columns (case-insensitive, flexible naming):

  • die_X / X coordinate — grid index, ≥ 0
  • die_Y / Y coordinate — grid index, ≥ 0
  • GDS-X — µm, absolute, origin at the die's own corner
  • GDS-Y — µm, absolute, origin at the die's own corner

Optional: ECID / CQC number — shown in hover tooltips on both maps.

Any other extra columns are kept and shown in defect tooltips. A row with a blank die_X/die_Y is dropped entirely; a row with a valid die but blank GDS-X/GDS-Y still counts on the wafer map but isn't plotted on the die defect map.

Runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves your machine.
